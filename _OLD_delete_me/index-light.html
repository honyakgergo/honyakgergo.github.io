A short introduction

The person behind the work.

Honyák / Portrait no. 04 / vol. I

I'm a second-year Applied Data Science & AI student at BUas, currently interning as an AI engineer on a small team that ships an internal RAG assistant. Most days I'm somewhere between research and plumbing, and I think that's the best place to be.

I started with classical ML on coursework that nobody wanted to do: emotion classifiers, plant-imaging research, a PID-tuning agent for a real robot. Each time I learned the same lesson the hard way: the model is the easy part. The honest one is the eval.

That stuck. Now I spend most of my time on quantitative ML: graph neural nets for cross-sectional equity factors, a momentum strategy trading real capital through SwingLab, and a live market dashboard that keeps me honest about what's actually happening in a session. I competed solo in IMC Prosperity 4 against 18,800 teams and finished 223rd overall, 1st in the Netherlands on the manual round.

Outside the laptop: preparing for a TU/e pre-master in Data Science & AI, and reading more Lopez de Prado than is probably healthy.
